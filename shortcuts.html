Shortcuts for your phone

Louder, without looking for the page.

A shortcut on your iPhone turns the Hasler up or down, plays a key or puts it to sleep: from the Lock Screen, the Action button, a tap on the back of the phone, or a word to Siri. No app, no account.

iPhone and iPad

Tap one, then Add Shortcut.

Each file opens in the Shortcuts app (iOS 15 or later). The first time one runs, Shortcuts asks to connect to hasler.local: tap Allow.

  • Hasler louderA little louder, never past the ceiling.
  • Hasler quieterA little quieter.
  • Hasler key 1Plays key 1, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler key 2Plays key 2, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler key 3Plays key 3, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler key 4Plays key 4, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler key 5Plays key 5, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler key 6Plays key 6, as if you pressed it.
  • Hasler standbySleeps, or wakes it again.
  • Hasler play/pauseStops, or plays the last station again.
  • Hasler next songThe next song of a playlist.

Where to put them

  • Siri: say the name, “Hasler louder”.
  • Back Tap: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap. Double Tap: Hasler louder. Triple Tap: Hasler quieter.
  • Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings → Action Button → Shortcut.
  • Control Center: touch and hold it → Add a Control → Shortcut.
  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → a button at the bottom → Shortcut.

The shortcuts call hasler.local, and they work on the home Wi-Fi, where the radio is. More than one Hasler? Open a shortcut in the Shortcuts app and change hasler.local to that radio’s name, for example kitchen.local.

Android

The radio has them itself.

On your phone, open the radio’s page (hasler.local) → Settings → Phones → Shortcuts for your phone. Open one, then in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. Its icon does it in one tap.

A Quick Settings tile or the volume buttons would need an app, and these use none. While your phone plays to the radio over Bluetooth or Spotify, its volume buttons already turn the radio up and down.
